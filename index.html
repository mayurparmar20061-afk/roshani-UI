What certifications will I receive?
You will receive an official, globally recognized training certification from Roshani Computer Institute. As an Autodesk Authorized Training Centre, our AutoCAD, Revit, and other technical certifications are accepted worldwide.
Does Roshani Computer Institute offer placement assistance?
Yes! We have a dedicated placement support cell that maintains active ties with over 500 engineering, design, and software firms across Gujarat and India. We provide resume building, mock interviews, and direct placement opportunities.
Are classes available in online or offline mode?
We offer both offline classes at our high-end Ahmedabad campus equipped with dual monitors and licensed software, as well as interactive live online batches with full mentor support.
Who will be teaching the courses?
Our faculty consists of Autodesk and Adobe certified professionals, industry specialists, and experienced engineers who have over 15+ years of active field experience.
